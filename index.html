Connect to n8n

Paste the Production Webhook URLs from your n8n workflows, then press Connect. After that, registrations and checks go to your Google Sheet.

To make the connection permanent, open this file in a text editor and set these two lines near the bottom of the file, then upload it again:

WEBHOOK_REGISTER_URL = "https://your-n8n.com/webhook/gym-register";
WEBHOOK_CHECK_URL = "https://your-n8n.com/webhook/gym-check";

Members are saved in the Gym System Google Sheet through n8n.
